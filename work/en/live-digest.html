Livestream Ops Digital Twin

2026.11 — presentBuildFor: the head of livestream operations in Vietnam

Built for the Vietnam livestream operations team. The pain: ops writes a report every day, but the material is scattered across several livestream group chats, TikTok dashboard screenshots and spreadsheets. They had to dig through each one, add up sales by hand and check by hand what changed that day. Finding and counting took the most time. I built a digital twin: it reads the group chats and screenshots for them, the numbers are computed by code, there's a daily digest every evening and a weekly report every week, and the person only checks.

How it works

Reads the groups, does the math, writes the summary
BeforeDig through each group chat, count by hand, write the reportAfterOne card at 21:30, one conclusion per item
01Read group chats🤖 AI
  • Reads the designated livestream group chats under the manager's authorization; no bot joins or posts, members notice nothing; reads text, rich posts and images
02Read dashboard screenshots🤖 AI
  • Reads GMV, units sold and views from TikTok LIVE dashboard screenshots; currency is taken as shown in the screenshot, flagged rather than guessed when unreadable; repeated screenshots of the same session count once
03Daily summary🤖 AI
  • One card at 21:30 every evening: sales overview, one block per brand, split into morning, afternoon and evening sessions; numbers computed by code, never estimated by the model; five fixed questions, one conclusion each, no timestamps or speaker names
04Weekly report🤖 AI
  • Every Saturday, the week by brand: sessions, GMV, units, one conclusion for the week
05Check👤 Human
  • Sent only to the manager, who checks it before reporting up; content is read and sent from the cloud on the spot, not stored long-term
