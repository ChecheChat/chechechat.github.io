KOL Digital Employee

2026.11 — in testingBuildExplainFor: the Thai creator BD team

Built for the Thai creator BD team. The pain: after a client joins the group chat, a complete brief takes three or four days of back-and-forth; screening creators and splitting lists takes hours per order; outreach runs on personal experience. KOLs are paid and negotiated one-on-one, so AI takes only the first half. Reviewing content, price negotiation and quotes stay with people.

Process: who does which step

🤖 AI digital employee · ✅ AI drafts, a person signs off before it moves on · 👤 Human

First half goes to the digital employee; judgment stays with people
BeforePeople collect and chase materials in the group chat, screen creators, split listsAfterAI runs the first half; people only spot check and judge
01Collect the brief🤖 AI
  • Client joins the group chat and gets a form; if incomplete, remind at most 3 times, as a notice, not a chase; still incomplete, hand to a person
02First screening of creators🤖 AI
  • Filter on the creator data platform by the brief's category, follower range and last 30 days GMV; client wants N, first screening returns 6N
03Dedupe, split lists🤖 AI
  • Match against the internal creator database, tag "worked with / contacted / new creator"; split evenly among people and send to the BD group chat
04Spot check✅ Human sign-off
  • Pull 5 per order and check the filter criteria; if wrong, give feedback and fix the rules
05Review content, outreach👤 Human + 🤖 AI
  • A person reviews TikTok content and marks fit or not; outreach and daily replies are done by AI under the person's account, in shadow testing: AI drafts first, compared against what the person actually sent; big gaps are reviewed at the weekly meeting and the rules updated
06Price negotiation, quote, execution👤 Human
  • Anything about price, deposits or contracts is negotiated by a person, who sends the quote; script localization, shooting and publishing are done by people; creator, outreach and quote records are written back to the creator database by AI, with a progress update posted once a day in the client group chat
