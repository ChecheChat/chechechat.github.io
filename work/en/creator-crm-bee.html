Creator CRM

2026.10 — presentBuildExplainFor: the Thai BD team + Chinese management

Built for the Thai BD team and Chinese management. The pain: the Thai team finds creators, sends them brand info and collects their submissions in Google Drive; Chinese management and Chinese brands work in Lark. The two sides don't line up, and management can't see the BD team's outreach status. I built an automatic sync: the Thai team keeps using Google, and a creator master table and dashboard grow in Lark on their own.

How it works

Thailand works in Google, China reads in Lark
BeforeDozens of project sheets scattered in Google; management can't see themAfterAuto-synced to Lark, one creator per row, the whole picture on a dashboard
01Thai team works as usual👤 Human
  • Creator info, brand project sheets and creator submissions all stay in Google Drive; no habits change
02Auto-sync to Lark🤖 AI
  • Folders, sheets, tabs and fields are copied over as-is, nothing added or removed, updated on a schedule; the original sheets are mirrored for reference
03Build the creator master table🤖 AI
  • Three linked tables: projects, creator master, project rosters. One creator per row; find people by category and size; collaboration count, scoring and blacklist at a glance; the follow-up stage is set automatically from what's filled in, no manual entry
04Management reads the dashboard👤 Human
  • Each BD's outreach funnel, effective outreach rate, successful collaborations, average score; before a creator is proposed, an automatic reminder of past collaborations and blacklist status
